## Conventions

- **Norm**: NFC + lowercase + ASCII-punctuation strip (Adja chars ɛ, ɔ, ŋ, ɖ
  and tone marks preserved). Use for cross-run ranking.
- **Raw**: exact-string comparison (conservative).
- **Median utt CER**: per-utterance median, robust to single-utterance
  Whisper hallucination loops that explode corpus-level WER >500%.

## Ceiling caveat

Our Adja ASRs are weak on real Adja speech (sanity-check normalized CER:
E4v4 ≈ 65%, C4v2 ≈ 70% on a 10-sample test slice). Reverse-WER on TTS
audio inherits that floor — treat numbers as a *relative* TTS quality
signal, not absolute intelligibility. The C4v2 column is more stable than
E4v4 because Whisper hallucinates on out-of-distribution audio (TTS noise,
unfamiliar acoustics) while CTC degrades gracefully. **Recommended
headline metric: C4v2 corpus-level CER (norm) + median utterance CER.**

- **CF2 still picks up the one intelligible sentence.** Even with the expanded run set, the CF2_adja_sent03 sample transcribes at 38% CER on C4v2 — the same sample the listening test flagged as "1/5 intelligible Adja." Sample-level reverse-WER is a useful drill-down even when corpus-level numbers are dominated by noise.
- **Stage 2 cross-language attempts (T1_csm_ewe_adja_stage2, T2_orpheus_*_ewe_adja_stage2*) all sit ≥100% CER.** This matches the listening verdict ("all noise except CF2 sample 03") and is the strongest quantitative evidence for the Gbe-family-cascade-doesn't-work-without-curriculum-annealing claim.

- **Whisper hallucination on E4v4 explodes some rows above 1000% CER** (notably T2_orpheus_en_lora_r128, T2_orpheus_fr_lora_r64). These are auto-regressive repetitive-token loops on out-of-distribution synthesis. Look at the C4v2 column for these runs; the median utterance CER is also more robust than corpus CER. This is the main reason we keep two architecturally different ASRs in the eval.
- **Skipped (no scoreable metrics.json):** T6 MMS-Ewe, T7 VoxCPM2, T10 F5, T11 E2 — all saved training config but no `generated` text↔audio map. T2 Orpheus zh-Ewe inference run had CUDA-error placeholders instead of audio. Future TTS runs should always emit a `generated` list of `{file, text, duration_sec}` records so reverse-WER can score them.
